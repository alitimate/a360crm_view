Launch Strategy Overview

Pre-Opening Masterplan Roadmap

A high-velocity, 4-stage launch framework built to convert local curiosity into long-term customer loyalty and sustained daily revenue in Khobar.

STAGE 01 • DAY -15 TO -1

Brand Setup & Curiosity

Asset finalization, digital footprints, tasting previews, and the sign-up wall to spark word-of-mouth anticipation.

STAGE 02 • GRASSROOTS

Pre-Opening Activations

Shift worker appreciation lunch, Golden Passes with tier-1 foodies, and key regional cultural community circles.

STAGE 03 • OPENING WEEK

Launch Stunt & Combos

The viral 1 SAR Biryani stunt for first 100 diners, live grilling theatre, and high-value family combo offerings.

STAGE 04 • FIRST 30 DAYS

Sustainable Marketing

Corporate office lunch delivery packages across Khobar business hubs, daily chef specials, and signature grill deals.

Phase 1 • 15 Days Before Opening

Brand Setup

Establishing bulletproof brand foundations, premium visual collaterals, and an active curiosity engine across digital and physical touchpoints.

📢

Brand Assets

  • ✓ Social media profiles setup: High-impact visual branding across Instagram, TikTok & Google Maps.
  • ✓ Printed flyers & QR menus: Premium tactile menus and frictionless scan-to-order QR codes.
  • ✓ Professional menu photo shoot: High-definition photography capturing the glistening rice, charcoal char, and rich textures.
🍴

Menus & Collateral

  • ✓ Digital & print menu finalization: Optimized pricing architecture, appetizing category hierarchies, and combo sets.
  • ✓ Shop & location reveal teasers: Behind-the-scenes venue previews spotlighting the Khobar storefront buildout.
  • ✓ Opening coupons & promo offers: Exclusive first-week incentive cards for neighborhood walk-ins and delivery.
🔥

Curiosity Engine

  • ✓ "Guess the Dish" social media campaign: Engaging mystery polls and ingredient reveals to spike algorithm reach.
  • ✓ "Be Among Our First 50" sign-up wall: High-intent VIP landing page to capture emails and mobile phone numbers.
  • ✓ Local foodie influencer tasting night: Private closed-door tasting for trusted Eastern Province food critics.
Phase 2 • Community & Grassroots Hype

Pre-Opening Activations

Activating grassroots goodwill, influential voices, and organic community networks before opening the doors to the general public.

🤝

Shift Worker Preview Lunch

Special lunch on Day -2 or pre-launch dedicated exclusively for delivery drivers, security guards, and neighborhood retail workers.

★ Impact: Prioritizing local heroes who become authentic, passionate word-of-mouth ambassadors across Khobar.
🎟️

Golden Passes & Influencers

Curated food influencers + 5 of their followers per session on Days 3, 5, and 7 of opening for exclusive behind-the-scenes content.

★ Impact: Guarantees continuous, compound viral media coverage across Instagram and TikTok for the first 10 days.
👥

Special Community Groups

Targeting regional expat and cultural circles (e.g. Malayali ladies group, INF and friends, Eastern Province community leaders).

★ Impact: Generates instant community trust, heartfelt advocacy, and a foundation of loyal, repeat family diners.
Phase 3 • Opening Week

Launch Event & Opening Combos

Creating an unforgettable launch spectacle with jaw-dropping promotional hooks, live street theatrics, and immediate viral social proof.

⚡

Digital Invites & 1 SAR Biryani

  • 1. Digital invitation drop: Synchronized release via regional micro-influencers & food WhatsApp groups.
  • 2. "First 100 Biryanis at 1 SAR" Stunt: The ultimate queue-generating headline offer to create massive, buzzworthy crowds on Day 1.
📍 Creates instant street-level FOMO and social media filming frenzy.
🎪

Opening Day as an Event

Transforming the storefront into a live sensory food celebration with live outdoor grilling stations, fragrant aroma wafting through the street, and rhythmic event energy.

🔥 Turns a standard restaurant opening into a must-visit Khobar food destination event.
🍱

Combos & Real Reactions

High-value opening week family meal combos paired with professional on-site videographers capturing unscripted, genuine customer tasting reactions.

🎥 Provides an endless library of authentic user reactions and social proof for ongoing ad campaigns.
Phase 4 • First 30 Days After Opening

Sustained Marketing & Growth Engine

Transitioning launch momentum into consistent high-volume revenue, corporate catering contracts, and weekly repeat dining habits.

🏢

Office & Group Meals

Targeted corporate lunch bundles formulated specifically for Khobar business districts, corporate towers, executive meetings, and large family gatherings.

💼 Locks in high-ticket recurring weekday B2B revenues from 12:00 PM to 3:30 PM.
🏷️

Signature Deals & Pricing

Weekly signature grill specials, weekday vs. weekend strategic value offers, and showcasing unbeatable meal value to outposition local competition.

📊 Maintains healthy margins while reinforcing Golden Biryani as the premier value champion.
👨‍🍳

Today's Special Menu

Rotating chef specials and limited-edition grill cuts displayed daily to encourage frequent repeat visits and continuous culinary excitement.

⭐ Gives regular diners a fresh reason to return 2-3 times every single week.
